The Colette Way

Colette brings a corner of Paris to Alpharetta. We source acclaimed teas and coffee from France, whisk ceremonial-grade matcha to order, and bake a fresh kitchen menu every morning — all designed to sit beside the desserts and pastries in our case.

Whether it is a quick espresso, a leisurely quiche, or a weekend crepe, every visit is meant to feel a little indulgent.
