Our Story

Colette was born from a simple love of the French cafe — the ritual of good coffee, the pleasure of a warm croque, and a pastry case that stops you at the door.

We serve acclaimed teas and coffee sourced from France, whisk ceremonial-grade matcha to order, and craft signature drinks from real fruit. Our kitchen bakes quiche, presses paninis, and plates fresh toasts and salads every day — with delicate crepes on weekends.

Everything on our menu is built to do one thing: pair beautifully with the desserts and pastries we are known for. Come sit a while. That is the whole idea.
