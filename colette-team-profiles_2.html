Colette · Team Profiles & Innovation Tracks

Everyone runs the store. Each one moves it forward.

Four people, one shared playbook — every person can open, serve, and close. On top of that base, each role owns 1–2 concentration tracks with a monthly cadence, so something new hits the counter every month and marketing always has something to promote.

☕ The shared core — everyone masters this

The base of every profile. Cross-training means any two people can run the store on any shift.
👥 All four team members
🌅 Opening Start of day
  • Set up all food & displays
  • Set up cleaning buckets
  • Equipment on — ovens, display cases, grill
  • Music on, open signs on
  • Umbrellas open (visible from the road), chairs & tables set outside
  • 5-min team huddle — today's specials, 86'd items, who's where ★ new
  • Taste the first espresso & check the case looks photo-ready ★ new
🤝 Front & service All day
  • Welcome every customer
  • Register — take orders, pack to-go, serve tables
  • Serve viennoiserie, bread & desserts
  • Make crêpes; warm sandwiches & quiches; prepare salads
  • Made-to-order: toasts, soup, parfaits
☕ Drinks All day
  • All coffee drinks — the heart of the shop
  • Special coolers & drinks, orange juice
  • Keep the cold self-serve stocked & fronted
🍳 Kitchen prep All day
  • Sauces & creams for sandwiches and salads
  • Build sandwiches in advance, ready to warm
  • Clean as you go — dishes, restroom, fold boxes, silverware, put-away
🌙 Closing End of day
  • Food away; displays, tables, floors & equipment cleaned
  • Coffee machine & bathroom cleaned
  • Curtains down, umbrellas closed, chairs up
  • All equipment off — ovens, crêpe grill…
  • Verify key refrigerators are running before leaving
  • Log what got tossed — waste tells us what to bake less of ★ new
  • Day-old plan — discount basket, staff take-home or donation ★ new
📲 Systems Weekly
  • Weekly buying list — via the app
  • Schedule — AI system, approve your shifts
  • Inventory — mostly automated, spot-check counts
💶 Cash & care Daily ★ new
  • Count the opening float; reconcile the till at close
  • Deposit prepped & sales vs. goal glanced daily
  • Backflush the espresso machine nightly; clean grinder weekly
  • Weekly deep-clean rotation — one zone per day (case glass, patio, fridge coils…)

🇫🇷 The French touch — sell the story, not just the food ★ Consultant's additions

You're not competing with other coffee shops on caffeine — you're selling twenty minutes in France. These cost almost nothing and are what people remember, photograph, and tell friends about.
👋 « Bonjour ! » standard
Every guest greeted in French within seconds of the door — and « Merci, à bientôt ! » on the way out. The accent is the ambiance.
🗣️ Product storytelling
Every team member can pronounce the viennoiserie correctly and tell one line of story — the butter, the layers, the how-it's-made. Train it like a drink recipe.
🖤 L'ardoise — the chalkboard
A hand-written daily board: today's special, the month's new item, a drink pairing. Changes daily, photographs beautifully, costs a stick of chalk.
🕓 Le goûter — own 3–5 pm
France's sacred afternoon snack becomes your slow-hours play: a pastry + coffee pairing at a set price. Turns the dead daypart into a ritual — and bridges into the new dinner hours.
🥐 Pairing suggestions
Gentle upsell built into service: "a macaron with that cappuccino?" Suggested pairings on the board and register — ticket size rises without pressure.
📅 The French holiday calendar
Galette des Rois in January, crêpes for La Chandeleur in February, Bastille Day in July, bûche de Noël in December — a year of built-in launches the tracks and marketing can plan around.

🔁 Every track runs the same monthly pipeline

This is the fix for "no consistency, no cadence, no planning, no connection to marketing." Tracks are staggered so something new launches every month — without everything landing at once.
Week 1
💡 Ideate
Pick the month's candidate — from customer feedback, seasons, trends.
Week 2
🧪 Test & taste
Make it, cost it, team tastes it. Kill it or green-light it.
Week 3
🎓 Train & prep
Recipe card written, everyone trained, supplies ordered via the apps.
Week 4
🚀 Launch + promote
Hits the counter — and Jared's engine promotes it: email, text & social, automatically.
